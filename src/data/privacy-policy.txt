## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Hacktrion.

Hacktrion is a platform designed to help students discover hackathons, connect with teammates, participate in events, showcase projects, and build their academic and professional portfolios.

This Privacy Policy explains how Hacktrion collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you access or use our website, applications, and related services.

Hacktrion is operated under the brand name **KodepiX**, an unincorporated startup team. References to "Hacktrion", "we", "our", or "us" in this Policy refer to the Hacktrion platform and its operator, KodepiX.

By using Hacktrion, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to information collected through:

- The Hacktrion website and associated services.
- User registration, login, and account management.
- Student profiles and preferences.
- Hackathon discovery, registration, and participation.
- Team creation and team communication features.
- Event announcements and notifications.
- Project submissions and showcases.
- Certificates and certificate verification.
- Support requests and communications with Hacktrion.

This Policy does not automatically cover third-party websites, platforms, or services that may be accessible through links on Hacktrion.

## 3. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, and improve Hacktrion.

### 3.1 Account and Registration Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

- Name or display name.
- Username and unique user ID.
- Email address.
- Login and authentication-related information.
- Profile photograph or avatar, if provided.
- Account preferences and settings.

### 3.2 Academic and Student Information

To support hackathon discovery and student participation, we may collect information such as:

- College or institution name.
- Course or degree.
- Semester and academic year.
- Skills and technical interests.
- Areas of interest.
- Experience level.
- Preferred event location.
- Preference for online or offline events.
- Preference for free or paid events.

You may choose which optional profile details to provide, where the relevant feature allows it.

### 3.3 Hackathon and Event Information

When you interact with hackathons or other events, we may collect:

- Events you view or save.
- Events you follow or request notifications for.
- Registration and application details.
- Participation status.
- Team information.
- Submission details.
- Event-related announcements and interactions.

Information submitted through an event application may also be shared with the institution or event organizer responsible for that event.

### 3.4 Team and Communication Information

When you create or join a team, use team chats, or interact with other participants, we may process:

- Team name and team membership.
- Usernames and user IDs.
- Messages and other information shared through team communication features.
- Team-related activity and event participation records.

Hacktrion is designed to use usernames and user IDs for team interactions rather than displaying members' private phone numbers or email addresses to other team members.

However, information that you voluntarily share in messages or other interactions may be visible to the intended recipients.

### 3.5 Technical and Usage Information

When you access Hacktrion, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

- IP address.
- Browser and device information.
- Operating system.
- Approximate location derived from technical information, where available.
- Access timestamps.
- Session and authentication information.
- Error logs, security logs, and diagnostic information.
- Platform usage and interaction data.

We use this information for security, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and service improvement.

### 3.6 Certificates and Project Information

Where applicable, Hacktrion may process:

- Project titles and descriptions.
- Uploaded submissions and supporting materials.
- Portfolio information.
- Certificate details.
- Certificate identification numbers.
- Certificate verification records.

Certain certificate details may be made publicly accessible through a certificate verification page so that authenticity can be checked.

### 3.7 Support and Communication Information

If you contact our support team, we may collect your email address, account information, message content, attachments, and other information necessary to understand and resolve your request.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

1. Create, maintain, and authenticate user accounts.
2. Provide access to Hacktrion's features and services.
3. Display relevant hackathons and opportunities.
4. Personalize event discovery and recommendations.
5. Enable event applications and participation.
6. Support team creation and communication.
7. Deliver event announcements, reminders, and deadline notifications.
8. Maintain saved hackathons and user preferences.
9. Process project submissions and showcase participation.
10. Generate and verify certificates.
11. Detect suspicious activities, spam, fraud, and misuse.
12. Maintain platform security and prevent unauthorized access.
13. Diagnose technical problems and improve platform performance.
14. Respond to support requests and legal obligations.
15. Enforce our Terms of Service and applicable platform rules.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of Hacktrion without an appropriate basis or notice, where required by applicable law.

## 5. Personalized Recommendations and Notifications

Hacktrion may use information such as your academic background, skills, interests, preferences, and activity on the platform to recommend relevant hackathons and opportunities.

For example, recommendations may consider:

- Your course and semester.
- Your skills and interests.
- Your preferred location.
- Online or offline event preferences.
- Free or paid event preferences.
- Your experience level.
- Events you have saved or interacted with.

We may also use event dates and participation information to send relevant reminders, announcements, and deadline notifications.

You can manage available notification preferences through the platform or your device settings, depending on the notification type.

## 6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

However, we may share relevant information in the following circumstances:

### 6.1 Institutions and Event Organizers

When you apply for, register for, or participate in an event, relevant information may be shared with the institution or organizer responsible for that event.

This may include your name, email address, academic details, application information, team details, and project submissions, depending on the event requirements.

Institutions and organizers are responsible for handling information they receive in accordance with applicable laws and their own privacy practices.

### 6.2 Other Hacktrion Users

Certain information may be visible to other users, depending on your profile settings and the features you use.

For example, usernames, profile details, team membership, public projects, and information you choose to share may be visible to other participants.

Do not share sensitive personal information publicly or in team communications.

### 6.3 Service Providers

We may share necessary information with service providers who help us operate Hacktrion, including hosting, authentication, infrastructure, security, email delivery, and notification services.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to us, subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

### 6.4 Legal and Regulatory Requirements

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable laws or lawful government requests.
- Respond to court orders or legal proceedings.
- Protect the rights, safety, and security of Hacktrion, its users, or others.
- Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
- Enforce our Terms of Service.

### 6.5 Business or Operational Changes

If Hacktrion undergoes a restructuring, transfer, acquisition, or similar operational change, relevant information may be transferred as part of that process, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

These measures may include:

- Access controls and authentication safeguards.
- Secure communication protocols where supported.
- Server and database access restrictions.
- Monitoring for suspicious activity.
- Security logging and incident investigation.
- Regular maintenance and security improvements.

However, no online platform or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and reporting suspected unauthorized account access.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, ensuring security, and meeting legal obligations.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

### 10.1 Team Chat Retention

Our intended retention policy is to delete team chat data from active systems within **three (3) days after the associated event concludes**.

This period may be extended where information must be retained for security investigations, legal obligations, disputes, or other legitimate requirements.

Copies in protected backups may remain until the applicable backup-retention cycle is completed.

### 10.2 Account and Event Records

Certain account, event, certificate, transaction-related, security, or participation records may be retained for longer periods where necessary for platform operations, verification, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

## 11. Account Deletion and Data Removal

You may request deletion of your Hacktrion account by contacting:

**support@hacktrion.app**

For account deletion requests:

1. Send an email from your registered email address.
2. Clearly mention that you want your Hacktrion account deleted.
3. We may request additional information to verify account ownership.
4. We will acknowledge your request and process it accordingly.

Our current target is to complete account deletion and the removal of associated personal information from active systems within **thirty (30) calendar days** after successful verification of the request.

Certain information may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Information stored in backups may be removed in accordance with the applicable backup-retention cycle.

Where appropriate, account access or public visibility may be restricted while a deletion request is being processed.
